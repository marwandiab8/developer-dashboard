For Ashley

of you.

What I see in you
This job didn’t create your value. It simply gave more people the chance to see what I have been lucky enough to see all along.
Your mind

You see things other people miss.

One of the things I admire most about you is the way your mind works.

You notice details. You think ahead. You see the practical solution before most people have even finished looking at the problem. You have this rare ability to make sense of things quickly, quietly and naturally.

The truckI can look at everything we need to fit in the back and say, “There’s no way.” You look at it for a second and say, “Yes there is. Do it this way.” And somehow, you’re right.
The patio stonesI’m thinking about how to level the stone in front of me. You’re already thinking about the step we might have missed and the problem it will create later.
Everyday lifeYou are observant, organized and practical in a way that constantly surprises me. You don’t just see a problem. You see the whole picture around it.
And yes, sometimes it almost offends me how quickly you see the answer—because I’m supposed to be the practical one.

But underneath the joke is something much bigger: admiration. I have spent most of my life solving problems, building things and figuring out how pieces fit together. So when you make me stop and think, “Damn, she saw that before I did,” I notice.

You do that to me all the time. And I love that about you.

What I’m grateful for

You make my life better just by being who you are.

I don’t say this enough, but I am deeply grateful for you.

I’m grateful for the way you notice things. For the way you organize chaos without making a big deal about it. For the way you think ahead. For the way you can challenge me, correct me, make me laugh, and somehow make me look at something differently.

I’m grateful for the life experience you bring into everything you do. For your judgment. For your common sense. For your ability to carry a lot and still keep moving.

Most of all, I’m grateful that I get to be beside you while you discover more and more of what you are capable of.

You have supported me, believed in me, encouraged me and made me feel seen. I hope this page gives a little bit of that back to you.
The part I want you to remember

Ashley, this is only the beginning.

For a long time, I think you underestimated yourself. Maybe you looked at where you had been, the time you had been away from work, or the things you thought you were missing, and you let those things speak louder than your actual ability.

I never saw you that way.

I saw your intelligence. Your life experience. Your judgment. Your ability to organize things, notice what everybody else misses, and solve real problems in a way that actually works.

I saw a woman who is incredibly capable, even when she didn’t always give herself credit for it.

And the part that makes me the proudest is not that I believed in you.

It’s that you started believing in yourself.

You stepped back into the world after being away from work for years and gave yourself permission to aim higher. You trusted that your intelligence, your experience and everything life has taught you mattered. You put yourself forward instead of holding yourself back.

That takes courage.

And now you have proof—not that you suddenly became capable, but that you always were.

When I got my job, you wrote me something that made me feel seen. You told me how proud you were of me, and I never forgot how that felt.

This is me trying to give that feeling back to you.

I see you.

I see how smart you are.

I see how capable you are.

I see how much you bring to the people around you.

I see how strong you are, even when you don’t realize it yourself.

And I see how much more there still is ahead of you.

I’m not proud of you only because you got the job. I’m proud of you because you challenged the limits you had placed on yourself—and chose to see what might happen if you stopped underestimating who you are.

I hope you carry this moment with you.

On the difficult days, I hope you remember that you belong there.

When you doubt yourself, I hope you remember that the people around you are only beginning to discover what I already know.

And when the next opportunity comes, I hope you never ask yourself, “Am I good enough for this?” before you ask, “Why not me?”

You deserve to be proud of yourself.

I am grateful for you. I admire you. I believe in you.

And I am more proud of you than I can properly put into words.

Love,
Marwan ♥
